# FLW Video Playground

## Purpose
This sandbox houses FLW-first video UI/UX experiments and companion engine adapter ideas without touching the core VDO Ninja experience.

## Ground Rules
- Keep prototypes light and intentionally outside the production code paths.
- Prototype in plain HTML/CSS/JS so the ideas remain fast to iterate.
- Capture learnings here so the team can promote successful patterns later.

## What we are trying to prove
1. Reliable 1:1 calls with FLW styling.
2. Scalable group calls and participant layouts.
3. Rich host controls (mute, kick, lock, screen-share, end call) aligned with FLW workflows.

## Subfolders
- `docs/` – decisions log, experiments checklist, and adapter plan.
- `ui/` – FLW-themed prototype shell with preview, participants, and controls.
- `engine-adapter/` – conceptual contract and notes for an FLW adapter.
- `assets/`, `mockups/`, `scripts/` – placeholders for future experimental materials.
